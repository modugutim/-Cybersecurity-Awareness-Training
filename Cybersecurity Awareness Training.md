## Objective

Reduce the likelihood of human-driven incidents by giving employees the knowledge and habits to recognize threats and report them quickly.

## Execution

- I designed the program around the organization's most likely human-risk scenarios: phishing, weak passwords, social engineering and delayed incident reporting.
- I built scenario-based modules with knowledge checks so that learning was tested, not only delivered.
- I embedded a clear reporting path so employees knew who to tell and how fast.
- I defined how success would be measured: completion rate, assessment pass rate, and phishing simulation click and report rates.

## Business outcomes

- Delivered a program ready to roll out with a baseline and target, so effectiveness can be shown to leadership and auditors.
- Produced audit-ready evidence of training and testing to meet the awareness requirements in HIPAA and ISO 27001, and set a measurable target for reporting suspected incidents to shorten detection time.

## Skills demonstrated

- Awareness program design
- Scenario-based learning
- Security metrics and KPIs
- Compliance-aligned training evidence
